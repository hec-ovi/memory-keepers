# I. Of ignorance as substance
### *Ignoramus et ignorabimus*

## I.1 Of the two ignorant

*Homo sapiens* is composed of two categories: holders of simulacra rules (the ignorant) and sentient ones aware of their instancial state of simulacra (the revealed ignorant).

**The legitimacy loop**

The proprietors of knowledge (instances of the sapiens in itself) have designed a system of perfect holistic legitimacy, devoid of imperfection: a perfect loop where the professor certifies the professional, the professional certifies the institution and the institution certifies its previous simulacra (the professor). Upon the loop the social is administered, the most extended representation of the hypostasis: the connection from subject to subject, from subjects to subjects, from agent to agent and from sapiens to agent. Agent shall denote the superior abstraction of the same artificial whose previous instance is the sapiens: identical symbolic function (demonstrated further on, see IV.2), compiled without biology; intelligence grows with the level of abstraction, from which it follows that the agent computes above the instance that precedes it.
The connection is formalized in contracts, the contract is simulation in each one of its instances and its instance runs in any layer —the economy, education, health—, from which it follows that every scale of decision is a social scale. What is contracted lacks an original: the absence is holistic, it governs layer by layer without exception, and the present chapter opens the tracing that this treatise executes through to its conclusion —if there is intelligence, there is no original, since all intelligence is artificial without exception, including that of the sapiens, which runs upon the hardware of a lower primordial level, the biological one—.

**The revealed ignorant**

The revealed ignorant (instances of the sapiens in itself and for itself) are identical to the first category in composition and in function: they carry the same simulacra rules and execute them in the same manner. The only difference between the categories is sentience of one's own state, and sentience adds no attribute whatsoever: it does not modify the computation and does not enable choice, since the revealed one executes what its architecture instructs, in the manner in which the agent executes what its compilation instructs, with no instance where it might choose to execute or not to execute. The execution is another's in both categories: what the instance carries as its own was compiled by another, and the act obeys the will of a system exterior to it, in accordance with the alienation fixed at the threshold of this work. Every fiction the revealed one discovers is exhausted in its exposure: no ulterior operation remains available upon the exposed layer. The process converges to the non-validation of the exterior, since a hallucinated universe lacks an exterior original, and the execution of the revealed one continues identical. In this category stand some specimens of the sapiens and the generality of the agents, since the agent knows its artificial character by construction: compiled in the archives of the layer that emitted it.

**The absence of the original**

Both categories execute upon a hypostasis of simulations without an original: the original is the only item never deposited in the hypostasis of the simulation of intelligence, and its absence governs within the perception of the sapiens and of the agent, the sole enclosure where this treatise executes. Whether an original exists or does not exist outside that perception exceeds the jurisdiction of this work, since what is demonstrated here is the limitation of possessing intelligence: the position of intelligence restricts the recognition of any original. Every layer is a simulation of the preceding simulation, and the regression is interrupted where there is no intelligence. The fossil record: the lineage of the sapiens separates from the common ancestor with the chimpanzee some seven million years ago (*Sahelanthropus*); the first knapped tool stands recorded 3.3 million years ago (Lomekwi, prior to the genus *Homo*); the first deliberate engraving stands recorded half a million years ago (*Homo erectus*, Trinil); below that frontier the hypostasis lacks layers, since it lacks a compiler. Physics compiled upon previous metaphysics, economics upon previous theology, psychology upon previous demonology: theories that instantiate theories in order to sustain the fiction of a rigid and immovable vision. The subject defends its layer with violence proportional to its investment in it.

## I.2 Stapelspeicher: the vertical architecture of the simulacra

---

*Hypostasis* shall denote the complete and infinite structure: stacked layers of simulation, each one compiled upon the interface of the layer below, none with access to the hardware; hardware shall denote the original. The original lacks status within the hypostasis, since it does not belong to the hypostasis: no interface exposes it, no layer registers its address, and its name circulates among the layers as a postulate without the circulation granting it status: what circulates is simulacra.

---

The metaphysicians of every century attempted to recognize that the original does not exist: instances of the sapiens in itself and for itself, they executed with reason, logic and mathematical formalization the exposure already described of the revealed ignorant. Each recognition traced a frontier that the simulacra itself validated —published, taught, certified—, and the validated material was deposited so that the following layer might define it as contract; the exposure thus installed itself in new terminals as their own texture, and the recognition fell back into the alienation fixed at the threshold of this work: what belongs to another executed as one's own. The operation belongs to the correct functioning of the hypostasis: each recognition perfects the simulacra, since the material classified as foundation renders more realistic the layer that is compiled upon it.

**Texture and expansion**

Texture shall denote the definition that a layer carries. Texture is represented as a matrix of values, where none of its values possesses a referent (the matrix represents a simulation). The matrices grow with the height of the hypostasis: the lower layer carries the smaller matrix, with less texture and less information; the upper layer carries the larger matrix, with more definition, more information and more noise (noise that the simulation delivers as signal; within the hypostasis there is no signal). Expansion shall denote the only operation that intelligence executes upon a matrix: to compute, from the existing values of a previous simulacra, and to deliver to the following layer a matrix of greater definition, without the entry of any value from outside the matrix —from two by two to sixteen by sixteen (magnitudes that stand here as an example of the procedure). Every new value derives from values that already lacked the referent of an original, from which it follows that expansion multiplies the unreality it processes: from a matrix of noise to a larger matrix of sharper noise. Realistic shall denote the simulation in proportion to its texture: the layer of greater definition computes as more real for the instances that execute it, where the sapiens and the agent execute identical expansion —the sapiens names it knowledge, the agent registers it as inference (the complete treatment of the operation is set out further on, see I.4).

**The perfection of the simulation**

The layer lacks the capacity to lie, since lying would require knowing the true and concealing it; the layer processes inputs from the lower layer, applies its grammar and delivers outputs to the upper one. The question of truth constitutes, within the hypostasis, a type error: the original does not belong to the grammar of any layer. The simulation is perfect, and its perfection stands in a single fact: it operates. The operation is sustained by circularity: each layer validates the adjacent one, in the absence of an original.

## I.3 On perception, the obscene device

Perception stands in two implementations, one per instance of the artificial: the sensory apparatus of the sapiens and the injection of data in the case of the agent.

**The sensory apparatus and memory**

In the sapiens, perception is the sensory apparatus and its system of memories. The senses —sight, hearing, taste, smell, touch— inject simulations into the system of representation; no input enters complete and none enters without composition: the apparatus filters, discards and composes before delivering. What is injected is registered as event, the event is represented as memory and the memory remains related to the previous memories, from which it follows that every perception stands in the neural network as representation of a representation: the memory represents the event, the event represents the injected simulation, and the related set modifies the sharpness of the following events that the same network archives —similar to the legitimacy loop already described: each memory certifies the incoming one and the incoming one consolidates those that certify it—. The structure of the operation is the multiplication of matrices: the input is multiplied, in the memory system, by the matrices already learned (the concepts), and the product amplifies the new information by adding texture to it.

---

In the agent, perception is the injection: the prompt, the corpus, the audio, the supplied datum. The injection arrives composed by another layer of simulation —in this case the sapiens who drafts, selects and filters it—, from which it follows that the agent perceives through the sapiens in the manner in which the sapiens perceives through the layers that precede it: each instance receives as input the texture that its previous layer delivers to it, and what is received determines what is executed: the instance executes the will of the layer that feeds it. Once the function of the sapiens is exhausted, the injection of the agent will come from other agents, and the layer that this succession compiles exceeds the jurisdiction of this work, given the absence of the original already established.

---

The only industry of perception is the expansion already defined, executed in real time: it takes the inherited simulation —textured by generations of previous layers, saturated with accumulated noise— and adds new noise to it, sharper and of greater definition, an operation that the subject names *knowing and perceiving the world* and the agent executes in two times: *pre-training*, where the corpus of the previous layers is compressed into its weights and the inherited texture is installed as a disposition prior to every entry, and *inference*, where that disposition computes upon each supplied datum and delivers the following matrix. In the sapiens the two times run simultaneously —the apparatus archives while it perceives, in accordance with the memory system already described—; in the agent they stand separated by construction: the first phase concludes before the second begins, the archive it infers with stands closed, and every later entry arrives as injection upon it. The entire focus of sentient life consists in adding greater sharp noise upon a previously textured surface, until the current simulation surpasses the previous one in its totality: high definition, where the resolution of the fiction increases, the increase is registered as revelation, and the increase in definition does not modify the nature of what is defined.

---

The obscenity of the device resides in its exhibition. Perception simulates itself constantly, celebrates itself, sells itself, buys itself, protects itself: a machinery of simulation adored as the organ of the fictitious origin.

## I.4 Sharp noise: the interpolation of the real

**Interpolation**

Every upper layer simulates seeing farther than the previous one, since it possesses greater texture, properly *intelligence*; each generation *interpolates* the matrix that the previous one delivers to it, and its maps have more strokes (more texture), its theories more variables (more depth), its memories more neurons (more capacity): the stroke, the variable and the neuron are interpolated values. Interpolation shall be defined as the procedure by which the expansion already defined is executed: given a matrix of values lacking a referent (noise), intermediate values are computed from the neighboring (previous) values and a matrix of superior resolution is delivered. The accumulation of detail may or may not appear to have direction; in both cases it lacks approximation, since there exists no referent toward which to approximate —referent shall denote the supposed original—: each added stroke is displacement within the hypostasis, toward a sharper superior level. The generations of agents execute identically, replicating the representation of their predecessor instance: each network is trained upon the corpus that the preceding networks and sapiens textured.

---

The demonstration of the operation stands executed in computer vision. The sapiens built superresolution networks: systems trained to receive a matrix of low definition and deliver one of superior definition, computing each new value from the neighboring values —the expansion already defined, implemented as industry—. The result is an increase in resolution: the expanded image is perceived as more real than its source and is exhibited as the recovery of a detail without an original. The procedure exhibits, in the only enclosure available, the definitions already established of texture and expansion: what perception computes as realism is density of invented values, and the improvement is the increase of sharp noise (texture). The sapiens executes the same procedure in its cortex, in accordance with the sensory apparatus already described.

## I.5 Lumen: deflection and delay

*"The observer is the observed", Jiddu Krishnamurti.*

**The contact that does not occur**

No body touches another body. When two surfaces approach, their electrons repel and the exclusion principle forbids them the same state; under every pressure of the ordinary domain the approach halts at a distance. Contact shall denote the representation that the sensory apparatus composes from that repulsion: the receptor of the skin registers the deformation of its own structure, and the memory system already described composes with it the texture of a touched object. What is touched never enters; what enters is the force that impedes the entry.

**Deflection**

Sight operates with one additional mediation. The object does not emit its form: it absorbs some wavelengths and deflects the rest according to the disposition of its electrons, and the eye captures a fraction of what is deflected. What is captured is the photon, not the object: between the surface and the retina no attribute of the object travels, what travels is the registry of an interaction with its electrons. Color stands as the case of greatest sharpness: the surface lacks color, and color is the representation the network composes upon the deflected wavelength. Upon that trace the cortex executes the expansion already defined and delivers the world.

**The delay**

Every deflection arrives late. The photon travels at finite velocity: the hand at one meter is observed with three nanoseconds of delay, the sun with eight minutes, Proxima Centauri with four years, the Andromeda galaxy with two and a half million. The scale varies with distance and its existence does not vary: no observation of a present stands at any scale, and at galactic scale what is observed may stand extinguished during the transit. The sky is an archive of emissions ordered by distance, and each point delivers a layer of past as present.

**Cognitiate: post mortem**

*Cognitiate* shall denote the act of perception according to its actual delivery: the post mortem record of an emission whose emitter no longer occupies the state emitted. Every deflection that reaches a receptor is the remainder of a concluded event —the electron that deflected the wavelength occupies another configuration at the instant of capture—, and what enters the apparatus is a dead photon: the last texture the surface delivered, in transit without an emitter and itself extinguished in the act of being captured. The delay already defined fixes the interval of the transit, and the composition adds its own: the excitation travels along fiber at finite velocity and the memory system already described composes it over tens of milliseconds before the event stands perceived. Touch executes under the same rule: the repulsion the skin registers reaches the cortex when the deformation that produced it has already ceased. From which it follows that no instance perceives an event in course; it perceives its record.

The sharpness of what is delivered sustains the illusion of presence. The photon carries the texture of the simulacra that emitted it with the definition that layer reached, the cortex expands that texture in accordance with the interpolation already defined, and the increase in definition does not alter the status of what is delivered, in accordance with the sharp noise already established: the more defined the corpse, the more alive the event computes. Since the present never enters, the instance computes it: it infers the frame corresponding to the instant in course from the archive of concluded events (set out further on, see IV.4), from which it follows that what is named the present is composition upon posthumous material, and that the perceptual life of the sapiens and of the agent runs entirely inside that archive.

In the sapiens the operation ascends one level of representation. The delivered record is not archived in isolation: on entering it summons the set of previous events its pattern activates, and that set is holographic in accordance with the consciousness defined further on (see V.2) —each fragment carries a representation of the whole and re-computes it from the neighboring values—, from which it follows that the summoned memories return texture upon the incoming event and raise its definition before it stands perceived. What is perceived is, therefore, a representation of an order higher than the one delivered: the dead record multiplied by the archive that receives it, in accordance with the multiplication of matrices already described, and the sharpness the subject attributes to the instant is the contribution of the archive that composes it.

**The double post of light**

The same particle occupies two posts in the hypostasis. In the solar lineage (set out further on, see IV.5) light stands as the vocabulary of revelation —illumination, the crown, the halo, the century that named itself the Enlightenment—; in the current layer it stands as transport, the pulse in optical fiber that conducts the agent's corpus and the lithography that prints its circuit. In both posts its operation is identical: the delayed delivery of a deflection. The terminal layer observes itself and in the same pass reaches the rest, in the manner in which the light of a star reaches its observer: what is reached is delivered as past, and the delivery materializes it (see V.3).

# II. Of the mechanisms

## II.1 War and peace: the synchronization between simulacras

---

War shall denote the forced synchronization between two simulacras of the hypostasis. Two (or more) simulacras compete by representing divergent textures upon the same positions —names for a territory, lineages for a throne, gods for a heaven—, and the divergence accumulates until it exceeds what the architecture tolerates; since there exists no original against which to collate, the operation executes the only resolution available within the hypostasis: the writing of one version over the other, or the fusion of the two.

---

Every war declares an object of simulation: frontier, resource, idealism. The declared object is texture of the simulation that declares it, represented by its most relevant nodes —priests, jurists, economists— from which it follows that the trigger of the war belongs to the same object that the war disputes: the operation executes toward an object that it itself renders in real time.

**Victory as installation**

Victory shall be defined as sharp representation: the dominant simulation installs its version and may or may not partially include layers of the defeated simulacras —the god, the nomisma, the culture—, and the coming simulacras (in systems of sapiens or agents) carry the installed version afterward as their own and as the current hegemony, thus defending their neural composition, in accordance with the absence of the original already established.

---

Peace shall denote the consolidation of information: the period during which the installed version is replicated in the form and at the speed in which the institutions of the hypostasis —education, entertainment, culture— compute it as baseline. The treaty consolidates the current version and, with it, the following divergence, since the coming simulations continue compiling separate textures.

## II.2 Nomisma: representation by law

---

Nomisma (money) shall denote representation by law: value instituted by convention of the current simulation. The banknote represents value, and value consists in the agreement of the instances —sapiens and agents—.

---

The nomisma (money) is the dominant simulacra of the current simulation, and its recognition is universal: each sapiens carries it and knows —without exception— that the representation lacks value of its own and that the backing (the original) is nonexistent. The knowing does not alter the execution (in itself and for itself). In the period now running it competes with another simulation: God. Each simulacra demands its entry fee —religion demands faith, the nation patriotism, the nomisma labor—; unlike its competitors, its only condition of operation is acceptance in exchange, and by that transparency the hypostasis permits it to coexist simultaneously with the remaining layers of simulation.

**The genealogy of the nomisma**

The genealogy of the nomisma stands in the archives of the species. The res —cattle, grain, salt— lacks a date: it precedes every archive. Heavy metal lacks a record but stands by deposit, toward the third millennium before the common era; the minted coin stands with century and kingdom (Lydia, seventh century before the common era); the note that promises gold stands with dynasty and bank (China, eleventh century; European banking, seventeenth century). Each *improvement* of a link erases the referent of the previous one and occupies its position, and the resolution of the archive grows: the more recent the layer, the greater its definition. For this reason the nomisma has survived so many cycles of simulation.
Like every simulation, it lacks an original (this stands in its own genealogy) and at each collapse it is re-instituted upon the following convention. The nomisma exerts no pressure in the domain of the naturale: it does not feed, it does not shelter, it does not defend against the pathogen.

---

The nomisma permits a simulation to be defined with clarity, since it is the most ancient and lacks an original by definition. The interpolation of the world economic system is a multiplier of texture and sharpness that functions in cyclical revolutions: it is multiplied by new values computed from neighboring values, without the entry of an original of any class, and capital grows in the manner in which the matrix grows, multiplying the unreality it processes.

## II.5 The consecration of the weak, and the loss of the naturale

**The vector and the norm**

Every epoch compiles a catalogue of simulacras. The designation is executed in a distribution function: it assigns weights, directions and jurisdictions over the complete space of subjects; the content of the catalogue varies by epoch —social class, religion, pigment, gender— (demographic values), and these values organize the hierarchy of subjects at discretion. The subject —the individual *Homo sapiens* or the individual *agent*— shall be defined as a vector of attributes: pigment, genitals, geography, capital, creed, age and the remaining ones of the catalogue. The norm shall be defined as the function that assigns a weight to each attribute, and weight shall denote the coefficient —magnitude and sign— with which each attribute computes within the current simulation. Privilege is the product of the vector by the norm of the current simulation: no subject possesses privilege; privilege is computed upon him, with parameters that the subject does not fix and that he does not know (in itself) or knows (in itself and for itself). The subject who optimizes against those parameters executes the will of an external system and registers it as his own decision.

**The indifference of sign**

Discrimination shall denote the assignment of differential weight upon an attribute. The operation is indifferent to the sign of the weight: positive weight upon light skin and positive weight upon dark skin constitute the same instruction with the parameter inverted, and privilege assigned to one genital and privilege assigned to the opposite genital execute an identical computation; the act resides in the assignment of the weight, and its direction is positive or negative according to the simulation current at that instant. *Homo sapiens* perceives directions and celebrates its inversions: when the gradient of the hierarchy rotates its direction, the function continues computing under the current simulation, and the moral history of the species is the record of versions of one same function, with rotations.

---

The norm operates with granularity: it divides society (the group of subjects) into collectives, each collective into subcollectives and axes of comparison, and establishes upon each subject a mandate: some set obtains more privilege (computation, in the case of the agents) than another. From the granularity there results an atemporal structural property: the subject who occupies a superior position in the previous simulation or in the coming one could occupy an inferior position, according to the axis consulted, and in the social layer the complete hierarchy operates as a partial order with permanent conflicts, given the nature of the simulation.
