## 1 (the post)

I built an AI with no chat box.

A 3D island where every layer of your life has its own keeper: an agent that writes your memories into books, answers only from what you actually lived, and dreams at night to connect them.

Google ADK + Gemini 3.5 on Vertex AI.

#AllThingsAgenticHackathon

https://youtu.be/2m9c1UvCAdA

## 2

Tell a keeper a memory and she reads the source herself, a talk, a song, a film, then writes it into a book in your own voice.

Ask her later and she opens her shelves. If it is not in a book she wrote, she does not answer it.

## 3

The part I like most: every night the island dreams.

A consolidation run links what your books share across every shelf, builds a knowledge graph, and births darker keepers from the patterns that keep returning.

Sit with one and it connects two of your memories, then asks you a question.

## 4

One switch changes the brain.

Gemini 3.5 on Vertex AI, or Gemma running entirely on your own machine. Same code, same island.

Your memories never have to leave your hardware.
